Ackvyn CRM

From first message to signed paperwork.

Live chat, tickets, contacts, documents, and PDF e-sign for the free Pages + git CMS world. The console and embeds come from this CDN; the API runs on a Cloudflare Worker you control.

What is Ackvyn CRM? Deploy your Worker Why it exists

CDN GUI

Console, chat widget, and docs — always current, no SPA copy on every client site.

Your Worker

Chat, email, and visitor intake on your Cloudflare Free Worker — console CRUD stays on GitHub where it can, like Sveltia.

Git records

Encrypted contacts, tickets, and Documents in crm-data/; public reviews JSON you can ship with the site.

Drop-in embeds

One script: src from this CDN, data-crm to your Worker.

One customer workspace

The useful parts of a CRM, connected

Conversations become tickets, tickets stay tied to contacts, and documents move through the same record instead of disappearing into another subscription.

Live chat & presence

A drop-in website messenger with WebSocket chat, visitor presence, notifications, transcripts, and agent handoff.

Tickets & intake

Turn chats, forms, and inbound email into an activity timeline with statuses, notes, attachments, macros, and assignment.

Contacts

Keep people, companies, ticket history, signed documents, and reusable details together. Import or export CSV when needed.

Document library

Store encrypted files in folders, prepare reusable PDF templates, retain signed copies, and link documents back to contacts and tickets.

PDF e-sign share links

Place signature, initials, date, number, text, checkbox, and dropdown fields; send a revocable link; attach the completed PDF and audit trail to the ticket.

Cryptographic proof

Signed PDFs include visual ink, a per-site self-signed PKCS#7 integrity seal, and an independently trusted DigiCert RFC 3161 completion timestamp.

Email & reusable workflows

Route inbound mail through your Worker, send from ticket context, and reuse response macros without moving customer history.

Analytics & reviews

Connect GA4 reporting, sync Google reviews to public JSON, and keep marketing embeds beside the customer workflow.

The real console

Same Messenger, Tickets, and Contacts panels as the live console — driven by a short scripted scenario. Use the timeline under the console to skip ahead. View-only; no Worker or sign-in.

This CDN

embed.js, product pages, docs, and the console GUI assets. Agents open the console from their operator site (iframe or link after sign-in) — not as a public marketing link.

Your Worker

Durable Objects, WebSockets, form intake, Email Routing — on your account. Agent ticket/contact edits prefer GitHub from the browser (usage notes).

Client sites

Marketing sites load the widget from here and talk only to your Worker.

Embed on a marketing site

<script
  src="https://crm.ackvyn.org/cdn/embed.js"
  data-site="your-site-key"
  data-crm="https://YOUR-SUBDOMAIN.workers.dev"
  data-mode="float"
  async
></script>

Never point data-crm at someone else’s Worker. Each operator deploys their own.
