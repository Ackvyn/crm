# Home

**Canonical docs live on the product site** (not only this GitHub wiki):

https://crm.ackvyn.org/docs/

Ackvyn CRM connects:

- Live website chat and visitor presence
- Ticket creation from chat, forms, and inbound email
- Contact history, CSV import/export, notes, and signed documents
- Encrypted document folders and reusable PDF field layouts
- Revocable e-sign share links with signature, initials, date, number, text,
  checkbox, and dropdown fields
- Completed-PDF audit hashes, a per-site self-signed PKCS#7 integrity seal, and
  an independently trusted DigiCert RFC 3161 completion timestamp
- Google reviews embeds and optional GA4 reporting

The e-sign timestamp proves when the completed PDF existed. The signer identity
certificate remains self-signed, not a public-CA or qualified electronic
signature. See [Documents & e-sign](https://crm.ackvyn.org/docs/documents.html)
for preparation, signing, trust boundaries, and verification endpoints.
